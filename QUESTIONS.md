# The Ten Questions — prototype

> Draft snapshot: 2026-09-27
>
> Assumption: Stage 0 has established a minimally reliable way to communicate and to interpret simple responses. Ask in order. Stop whenever necessary.

These formulations are intentionally provisional. The Japanese and English lines are parallel working expressions; neither is canonical.

## 1

**これは、あなたにとって私たちとの最初の接触ですか。**  
**Is this your first encounter with us?**

Why: whether the other side already knows us changes almost every question that follows.

## 2

**あなたは、一つの存在ですか。**  
**Are you one entity?**

Why: do not assume an individual speaker. The counterpart might be collective, distributed, planetary, artificial, or something for which our singular/plural distinction fits poorly.

## 3

**あなたと同じような存在は、他にもいますか。**  
**Are there others like you?**

Why: this begins to test whether anything resembling peers, a kind, a population, a species, or a civilization exists on the other side.

## 4

**私たちは、複数の存在だと理解していますか。**  
**Do you understand us as more than one entity?**

Why: before relying on *we*, *humanity*, or collective intention, check whether the counterpart recognizes plurality on our side.

## 5

**あなたは、他の存在を代表して話していますか。**  
**Are you communicating on behalf of others?**

Why: the communicating entity and the community, network, institution, or collective behind it may not be the same thing.

## 6

**この接触は、あなたの側で意図されたものですか。**  
**Was this encounter intentional on your side?**

Why: distinguish deliberate contact from accident, discovery, interception, observation, or coincidence without assuming which side travelled.

## 7

**あなたは、出来事に前と後があると認識しますか。**  
**Do you distinguish before from after?**

Why: establish whether ordered events are a shared concept before relying on familiar notions of history, prediction, waiting, or sequence.

## 8

**あなたは、ある出来事が別の出来事を引き起こすと認識しますか。**  
**Do you recognize that one event can cause another?**

Why: temporal order and causality are not the same. Shared causal reasoning matters for explanation, prediction, responsibility, and agreements.

## 9

**あなたは以前に、自分とは異なる知的存在と接触したことがありますか。**  
**Have you previously encountered an intelligence unlike yourselves?**

Why: this may reveal whether first contact is new only to us, and whether other forms of intelligence are already known to the counterpart.

## 10

**私たちが今、あなたに尋ねるべき、もっと重要な質問がありますか。**  
**Is there a more important question we should ask you now?**

Why: the list itself is a human artifact. The final question gives the other side an explicit opportunity to expose a blind spot in our framing.

---

## Open problems in this prototype

The current list is deliberately unstable. Among the unresolved questions:

- Are time and causality important enough to occupy two of only ten slots?
- Should willingness to continue communication appear earlier?
- Should safety, intervention, or potential harm be asked explicitly, and if so only after intention and causality are better grounded?
- Does question 1 mean *us*, *humanity*, *Earth-origin intelligence*, or merely the current communicating party?
- Are questions 2–5 too dependent on concepts of entity, representation, and plurality that Stage 0 may not yet support?
